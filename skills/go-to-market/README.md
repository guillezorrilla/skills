# go-to-market

Finding target accounts and writing the first message to them. Sales work, none of it needing a terminal.

One rule runs through all of them: every fact that reaches a prospect or a customer carries a source and a date, or it does not get sent. Invented familiarity is how AI-written outreach fails, and it fails expensively, because the reader is the one person who can check the claim instantly.

**Model-invoked**

- [`icp-lead-generation`](./icp-lead-generation/SKILL.md) finds target accounts against a profile the team already trusts, with filters that can come back false and a citation per row
- [`cold-outreach`](./cold-outreach/SKILL.md) writes a first touch on one dated fact, with a single small ask and a compliance floor

They chain in that order, and each works alone.
